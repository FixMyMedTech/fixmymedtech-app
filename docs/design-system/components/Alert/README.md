# Alert

The result of what the person just did, or something they must know before acting. Say what happened and what to do next.

- `alert-ok` after a save, `alert-danger` when an action failed, `alert-warn` for a risk to patients or equipment, `alert-info` for a pending step.
- At the top of the form or card it concerns.

**What the consumer provides:** one or two sentences (i18n keys).

# Hero

A gradient panel that greets the person and shows the state of their world in "pieces": big numbers that are also links. Used on the dashboard and home page; `.cj-hero` is the community variant.

- Background `--gradient-brand`, radius `radius-panel`, the white Africa logo as a soft watermark top-right.
- Eyebrow (12px uppercase), `h1` 26–38px, one line at 16px. A white `.btn-light` for the one action.
- Optional `.meter`: share of working devices, white on 25% white.
- `.pieces`: 2–5 translucent tiles, each a white dot with a status-coloured icon, an 800-weight number and a short label. Each opens the filtered list.
- Never put small or muted text on the teal half.

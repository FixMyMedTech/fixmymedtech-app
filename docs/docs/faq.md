# Questions and problems

<div class="in-app all" markdown>**In the app:** the **?** button at the bottom right of every page lets you **Send feedback** or **Report an issue** if your question is not here.</div>

## Getting in

### I don't see the Work tab

The tabs **Equipment**, **Work** and **Community** appear when your three welcome steps are done. Until then you are a **Visitor**: you see only **Getting started** and **Library**, and every other page brings you back to **Getting started**.

Open **Getting started** → **Start the welcome quests** and check which step is still **To do**: **Complete your profile**, **Add a photo** or **Attend a community talk**.

Clinic accounts and clinic staff never see tabs: they have one menu (**Dashboard**, **Devices**, **Faults**, **Groups**, **Library**, **Tasks**, **Network**). See [How the app is organised](three-worlds.md).

### I attended the talk but I am still a Visitor

The host must tap **Confirm talk attended** after the call, and your profile and photo must be done too. Your profile needs a name, a country, a city and a few words about you. Then open any page: you see *"Welcome to the community!"*.

### I didn't get the email

- Look in your **spam** or **promotions** folder.
- **Password reset**: on the sign-in page tap **Reset password**, type your email, tap **Send reset link**. The link works for 60 minutes.
- **Verification**: on **My Profile**, tap **Send verification email**.
- **Invitation from an engineer or a clinic**: ask them to send it again. An invitation link works once; if it says *"This invitation has already been used"*, just sign in.
- **Added by your clinic**: ask your clinic administrator to check your email address in **Groups**, remove you and invite you again.

### "An account already exists for this email"

Sign in with that account first, then open the invitation link again: you can accept it with your current account (**Accept the invitation**).

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in page: **Have you forgotten your password?** → **Reset password**.

## Devices

### Why can't I register a device?

The page *"You can't register a device yet"* lists your clinics and the reason for each:

| The app says | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"Registering devices unlocks when your three welcome steps are done."* | You are still a Visitor | Finish your welcome steps |
| *"named you; finish your welcome steps"* | The clinic named you, but your steps are not done | Finish your welcome steps |
| *"answered "not now"; it can confirm you later"* | The clinic did not confirm you | Ask it to tap **Confirm** in its **Engineers** tab |
| *"waiting for its clinic talk"* | The clinic signed up without an invitation and its talk is not confirmed yet | The clinic books its talk; a Clinics Connector unlocks it |
| *"No clinic lets you register its devices yet."* / *"has not named you"* | No clinic named you | [Invite a clinic](invite-clinic.md), or ask a clinic to add you as an engineer |

The rule: you can register devices for a clinic when it names you one of its responsible engineers and your welcome steps are done, or when you administer it; and in both cases only once the clinic is active.

### My clinic is waiting for its clinic talk

A clinic that signs up without an engineer's invitation (or is created from **Groups**) waits for a short **clinic talk** with the community. Until a Clinics Connector unlocks it, **nobody can register its devices, its own administrators included**.

- Book the talk: **Set up your clinic → Clinic talk → Book the clinic talk**. No link? The community contacts you.
- Faster: ask an engineer you know to invite your clinic. Invited clinics don't wait.

### "Scan a QR code" says I can't register a device

The **Scan a QR code** button on **Home** is for registering new devices. To open a device that is already registered, scan its sticker with your phone's camera app, or open it from **Devices**.

### Who can see my devices?

- **Anyone with the QR sticker**: the public page (name, brand, serial number, status, service date, manuals, open faults). No private clinic details.
- **The private device page**: your clinic's members, the organisation that maintains the device, the person who registered it, and your confirmed responsible engineers.
- **Engineer profiles**: one line per device they registered. Your administrators can hide it, or hide your name, in **Groups → your clinic → Privacy**.

See [Look after your clinic's devices](clinic-devices.md#who-can-see-your-devices).

### How do I delete a device?

**Devices** → **⋯** on its row → **Delete**. Only the clinic's administrators and the person who registered it can. Uploaded documents stay in the library.

## Faults and repairs

### I can't report a fault: it asks me to sign in

Reporting a fault needs an account. Ask your clinic to add you (**Groups → Invite user**), or create an account.

### Nobody answers my call for an engineer

Your responsible engineers are told first. After 20 minutes (**Device stopped**) or 4 hours (**This week**), engineers in your city are told too. Check that your clinic has responsible engineers (**Engineers** tab) and that its region and address are filled in.

### How much does it cost?

The app is free. Repair fees are agreed between the clinic and the engineer and paid outside the app: the app only writes them down.

### I can't send offers

Only engineers whose welcome steps are done (Contributors) send offers, and only for requests they were told about.

## Settings

### How do I change the language?

At the bottom of the menu, choose **EN**, **ES** or **FR**. On a phone, tap **☰** first. On public pages (QR page, sign-in), the language buttons are at the top. The app remembers your choice while you are signed in on that phone or computer.

### How do I switch to dark mode?

The moon button at the bottom of the menu.

### How do I become a talk host?

Talk hosts are members of the Community Builders (community talks) or of the Clinics Connectors (clinic talks). Ask a Community Builder to make you a candidate for one of these teams. See [How the community works](community-guide.md).

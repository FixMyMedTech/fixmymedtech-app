# Glossary

The words the app uses, in alphabetical order.

Achievement
:   Something you do for the community, counted in stars (10 levels). Five exist: Mapper, Ambassador, Supporter, Event Organiser, Active Builder. See [How the community works](community-guide.md).

Applicant
:   The word used in **Community admin** for a Visitor whose welcome steps are not done.

Candidate
:   First level in a team. A Community Builder makes you a candidate; the team's lead then makes you a member.

Claim
:   Your request to have a recognised achievement counted ("I organised an event"). Your team confirms it. See [Claims, task board and missing manuals](community-work.md).

Clinic
:   A hospital, clinic, health centre or laboratory on FixMyMedTech. Its people see its devices; its administrators manage it.

Clinic account
:   An account created to set up a clinic (from an invitation or at **Sign up your clinic**). It sees one Equipment menu and needs no welcome steps.

Clinic talk
:   A short call between a clinic that signed up without an invitation and the community. Until a Clinics Connector unlocks the clinic after it, nobody registers its devices.

Community talk
:   A short video call between a new engineer and a community member. It is the third welcome step.

Contributor
:   An engineer whose three welcome steps are done. Shown as **Account status: Contributor**. All three worlds are open.

Fault
:   A problem reported on a device. It goes through *Reported → Assigned → Diagnosis and quote → Approved → Repaired*. See [Report a fault](report-fault.md).

Group
:   An organisation you belong to (a clinic, hospital or maintenance organisation), listed in **Groups**, with your role in it.

Healthsite
:   The clinic or hospital where a device sits. Many come from [healthsites.io](https://healthsites.io), an open map of health facilities.

Lead
:   Highest level in a team. Adds and removes members, edits the team's page, confirms claims alone. Named by superusers.

Library
:   All the manuals and guides in FixMyMedTech and on iFixit, searchable by device, brand or model.

Maintenance organisation
:   The organisation that maintains a device (the **Responsible organization** chosen when registering it). Its administrator assigns faults.

Member
:   A person in a team, after being a candidate. (The app also writes "Member since" on **My journey** for the date you became a contributor.)

Offer
:   An engineer's answer to a request: a visit fee and an arrival time. The clinic accepts it or asks once for less. See [Requests and offers](requests.md).

QR page
:   The public page of a device, opened by scanning its QR sticker. Anyone can see it, signed in or not.

QR sticker
:   The label with a QR code stuck on each device. It links the device to its page.

Recognised achievement
:   An achievement the app cannot count by itself; a team member confirms it.

Request
:   A clinic's call for an engineer on a broken device (**Call an engineer**). Engineers see it in **Requests**.

Responsible engineer
:   An engineer named by a clinic (confirmed in its **Engineers** tab). Can register the clinic's devices and see their private pages, once their welcome steps are done and the clinic is active.

Responsible organization
:   See *Maintenance organisation*.

Severity
:   How bad a fault is: Low, Medium, High, Critical. High and Critical put the device out of service.

Staff
:   People added by their clinic (nurses, doctors, technicians, administrators). They don't need the welcome steps.

Talk host
:   A Community Builder or Clinics Connector who confirms community talks or unlocks clinics, on the **Talk host** page.

Task
:   Two meanings. In **Tasks** (**My Tasks**): the work assigned to you (repairs, services) and open requests. On the **Task board**: a small community job worth points.

Team
:   Communicators, Community Builders or Clinics Connectors. Each has candidates, members and leads.

Visitor
:   A new engineer whose welcome steps are not done. Sees only **Getting started** and **Library**.

Welcome steps (welcome quests)
:   The three steps that make a Visitor a Contributor: complete your profile, add a photo, attend a community talk.

World
:   One of the three parts of the app: **Equipment** (blue), **Work** (teal), **Community** (purple). See [How the app is organised](three-worlds.md).

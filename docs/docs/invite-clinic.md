# Invite a clinic

<div class="in-app work" markdown>**In the app:** <span class="world work">Work</span> → **Invite a clinic**.</div>

**Who this is for:** engineers whose welcome steps are done (Contributors).

**What you need before:** the email address of someone at the clinic who can set it up (the administrator, the head nurse...).

When you invite a clinic, you stay recorded as the engineer who brought it in, and the clinic skips the clinic talk.

## Steps

1. Open **Work → Invite a clinic**.
2. Under **New invitation**, type the **Clinic contact email**. Add a **Message (optional)**: a few words from you.
3. Tap **Send invitation**. The clinic gets an email that explains FixMyMedTech, with a link to set up its clinic.
4. Follow it under **Sent invitations**:
    - **Sent**: the email is gone;
    - **Account created**: the clinic created its account;
    - **Clinic set up**: it found or created its clinic;
    - **Confirmed**: it answered **Yes** to *"Do you confirm that {you} can register devices in the name of {clinic}?"*;
    - **Not now**: it did not confirm you yet. It can do it later from its **Engineers** tab.
5. **Your clinics** lists every clinic that named you, with what you can do there: **Can register**, **Waiting for welcome steps**, **Waiting for clinic talk** or **Not now**.

## What happens next

Once the clinic confirmed you, register its devices: tap **+ Register a device** (top right) or see [Register a device](register-device.md).

What the clinic does on its side: [Set up your clinic](clinic-setup.md).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Finish your welcome steps first."** Inviting clinics opens when your three welcome steps are done.
- **"The invitation could not be sent."** Check the address and try again.
- **The clinic never got the email.** Ask them to look in their spam folder. The link in the email is their way in; send a new invitation if needed.
- **The clinic answered Not now.** Talk to them: they can confirm you any time from **Groups → their clinic → Engineers → Confirm**.
- **The clinic already had an account.** If the clinic already exists with an administrator, the new account cannot take it over (*"This clinic already has an administrator"*). Ask that administrator to add you as an engineer from the **Engineers** tab.

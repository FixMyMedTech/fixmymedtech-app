# Talk host

<div class="in-app work" markdown>**In the app:** <span class="world work">Work</span> → **Talk host** (for clinic accounts: in the single menu).</div>

**Who this is for:** members and leads of the **Community Builders** (community talks) and of the **Clinics Connectors** (clinic talks).

**What you need before:** your place in one of those two teams, and a video call tool to hold the talks.

Two kinds of talk open things in the app:

| Talk | With whom | What it opens | Who confirms it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Community talk** | A Visitor (new engineer) | Their third welcome step. With the other two done, they become a Contributor | Community Builders |
| **Clinic talk** | A clinic that signed up without an invitation | Device registration for that clinic (its administrators and its engineers) | Clinics Connectors |

## Steps

### Confirm a community talk

1. Hold the talk: a short video call to welcome the Visitor and explain how the community works.
2. Open **Talk host**. Under **Visitors doing their welcome steps**, each row shows the three steps (**Complete your profile**, **Add your picture**, **Attend a community talk**) with **Done** or **To do**.
3. Tap **Confirm talk attended** on their row.

The same can be done from **Community admin → Applicants** with **The call took place**.

### Unlock a clinic

1. Hold the clinic talk with the contact shown under **Clinics waiting for their clinic talk** (name and email).
2. Tap **Talk done, unlock**.

## What happens next

- **Visitor**: if their profile and photo are done too, they are a Contributor at once. Next time they open a page they see *"Welcome to the community!"*.
- **Clinic**: its administrators and its confirmed responsible engineers can now register its devices.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Visitor is still a Visitor.** Their profile (name, country, city, a few words about them) or their photo is missing.
- **A clinic I talked to is not in the list.** Invited clinics never wait for a talk: they are active from the start. A clinic is in the list only when it signed up without an invitation, or was created from **Groups**.
- **I don't see Talk host.** You need to be a member or lead (not a candidate) of the Community Builders or Clinics Connectors.

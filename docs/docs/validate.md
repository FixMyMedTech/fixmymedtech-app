# To validate

<div class="in-app community" markdown>**In the app:** <span class="world community">Community</span> → **To validate**.</div>

**Who this is for:** members and leads of a team (Communicators, Community Builders, Clinics Connectors).

**What you need before:** you are a **Member** or **Lead** of a team, and you were active on the platform in the last six months.

**To validate** gathers the work that waits for a check: achievements people claimed, and tasks marked done on the task board.

## Steps

### Validate a claim

1. Open **To validate**. **Claims to validate** lists the claims for your team's achievements, with the note and the proof photo.
2. Read what happened. Then:
    - **Confirm** (leads): the claim is confirmed at once.
    - **I agree** (members): your vote counts as one of two. A second member must agree too.
    - **Ask for more**, with a short note: the claim goes back to its author.
    - **Decline**: the claim is closed.

You never validate your own claim.

### Check a task

1. **Tasks to check** lists tasks marked done.
2. Look at the note or link. Tap **Looks good** (the points go to the person who did it) or **Send back** (the task returns to them).

The task's author or a team lead checks it; the person who did the task never does.

The shortcuts at the top lead to **Talks** (for talk hosts), **Team admin** and the **Task board**.

## What happens next

A confirmed claim adds to the person's achievement count, and may give them a new star. They see it on **My journey**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Join a team to validate its achievements."** You are not a member of a team yet. Ask a Community Builder to make you a Candidate, then the team's lead.
- **I don't see a claim I expected.** You only see claims for your own team's achievements, and never your own.
- **I don't see To validate in the menu.** Candidates and paused accounts (no activity for six months) don't have it. Any action in the app restarts your team powers.
